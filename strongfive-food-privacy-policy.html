StrongFive Food Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

StrongFive Food is developed by Ice Digital. Your privacy is important to us.

In short: there are no accounts, ads or tracking. Your food diary stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. The app only sends something to anyone else when it needs Open Food Facts: a barcode number when you scan a product it doesn't already know, or your search words when you tap Search online.

Data Collection

StrongFive Food does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There is no account to create. Everything you enter (foods, recipes, saved meals, your food diary, flex days, goals and weight) is stored on your device and synced to your personal iCloud account. Ice Digital has no access to it.

Food Search

When you type in the search box, the app searches your own foods and the common foods built into the app (from Health Canada's Canadian Nutrient File and USDA FoodData Central). This happens on your device and doesn't use the internet.

Packaged foods aren't built in. To find one, you can tap Search online (or press Search on the keyboard). Only then does the app send your search words to Open Food Facts, a free, community-run food database, to search for products sold in Canada, or in other countries if you ask for them. Opening a result then asks Open Food Facts for that product by its barcode.

Barcode Lookups and Online Search (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode for a product that isn't already saved in the app, StrongFive Food asks Open Food Facts for that product's nutrition information. A barcode request contains only the barcode number; an online search contains only your search words. Both include the app's name and version. Neither includes your name, your diary, your health data or any identifier for you or your device. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts can see the IP address it came from. Their handling of that data is covered by the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Products you look up are saved on your device, so scanning the same product again doesn't contact Open Food Facts. Recent online searches are remembered on your device for a few minutes, so repeating one doesn't send it again.

Camera and Photos

StrongFive Food uses the camera to scan barcodes and, when a product isn't found, to read the nutrition label so you don't have to type it. You can also pick a photo of a label from your photo library. Barcodes and label text are recognised on your device. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the app may use Apple's on-device model to read numbers the app couldn't read itself; this also happens on your device. Camera images and photos are not saved or uploaded.

Apple Health

With your permission, StrongFive Food connects to Apple Health:

  • Reads your body weight and active energy, to show your weight trend, your activity for the day and, if you turn on Add Exercise Calories, to add it to that day's calorie goal.
  • Reads your resting energy, to show the energy you burned each day in Trends. It is not stored by the app.
  • Reads your height, date of birth and sex, only to suggest a daily goal in the goal calculator. These are not stored by the app.
  • Writes the calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat you log, the fibre, sugars, sodium, saturated fat, cholesterol, potassium, calcium, iron and caffeine in it when the food lists them, and weights you enter in the app.

You choose exactly what to share in the Apple Health permission screen, and you can change it any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps. Health data is never sent to Ice Digital or any third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing.

iCloud Sync

The foods, recipes, saved meals, diary, flex days, goals and weights you enter in StrongFive Food sync between your iPhone and Apple Watch using your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). This data is stored in your iCloud account, not on servers run by Ice Digital, and is governed by Apple's iCloud privacy policy. Data read from Apple Health is not copied to iCloud; each device reads it from Health directly. Settings such as your weight units, Save to Apple Health and the details you type into the goal calculator stay on the device where you entered them. One setting, whether Add Exercise Calories is on or off, is kept in your iCloud account's key-value storage so your iPhone and Apple Watch agree; nothing else is stored there. Settings in the app shows whether iCloud sync is on. You can turn off iCloud for StrongFive Food in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Apple Watch, Widgets and Complications

The Apple Watch app uses the same iCloud data as your iPhone, and reads Apple Health on the Watch itself. To keep your watch face up to date, your iPhone also sends today's summary (calories and protein eaten, your calorie and protein goal, a flex day's extra calories and, if you turned on Add Exercise Calories, the day's active energy) straight to your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity, over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. It doesn't go to Ice Digital or any other server.

Widgets on your iPhone and complications on your Apple Watch show the same summary. So they can show it without opening your diary, each device keeps a copy in a storage area shared only by StrongFive Food and its own widgets (an App Group). This copy isn't synced to iCloud or sent anywhere. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen or watch face can see what a widget or complication shows; remove it if you prefer.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can log food, log a saved meal or ask how many calories you have left with Siri or Shortcuts. Apple's Siri handles the request; the app then logs it or answers on your device, the same as if you'd done it in the app.

Exporting Your Data

Export Data (in Settings) makes spreadsheet files of your diary, weights (including those read from Apple Health), foods, goals and flex days on your device; an export only goes where you send it from the share sheet, and nothing is sent to Ice Digital or anyone else.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting a food, saved meal, flex day or diary entry in the app deletes it from your devices and iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Data StrongFive Food saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Analytics & Tracking

StrongFive Food contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, no advertising frameworks and no tracking. The app's only network connections are to Apple's iCloud and to Open Food Facts, for barcode lookups and when you tap Search online. Your iPhone also talks directly to your own paired Apple Watch, as described above.

Food Data Credits

Children

StrongFive Food is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
