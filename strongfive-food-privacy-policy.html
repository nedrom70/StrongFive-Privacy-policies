StrongFive Food Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

StrongFive Food is developed by Ice Digital. Your privacy is important to us.

In short: there are no accounts, ads or tracking. Your food diary stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. The only thing the app sends to anyone else is a barcode number, when you scan a product it doesn't already know.

Data Collection

StrongFive Food does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. There is no account to create. Everything you enter (foods, recipes, your food diary, goals and weight) is stored on your device and synced to your personal iCloud account. Ice Digital has no access to it.

Barcode Lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode for a product that isn't already saved in the app, StrongFive Food asks Open Food Facts, a free, community-run food database, for that product's nutrition information. The request contains only the barcode number and the app's name and version. It does not include your name, your diary, your health data or any identifier for you or your device. As with any internet request, Open Food Facts can see the IP address it came from. Their handling of that data is covered by the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Products you look up are saved on your device, so scanning the same product again doesn't contact Open Food Facts. Food searches are done on your device.

Camera

StrongFive Food uses the camera to scan barcodes and, when a product isn't found, to read the nutrition label so you don't have to type it. Barcodes and label text are recognised on your device. Camera images are not saved or uploaded.

Apple Health

With your permission, StrongFive Food connects to Apple Health:

  • Reads your body weight and active energy, to show your weight trend and your calories for the day.
  • Writes the calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat you log, and weights you enter in the app.

You choose exactly what to share in the Apple Health permission screen, and you can change it any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps. Health data is never sent to Ice Digital or any third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing.

iCloud Sync

The foods, recipes, diary, goals and weights you enter in StrongFive Food sync between your iPhone and Apple Watch using your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). This data is stored in your iCloud account, not on servers run by Ice Digital, and is governed by Apple's iCloud privacy policy. Data read from Apple Health is not copied to iCloud; each device reads it from Health directly. You can turn off iCloud for StrongFive Food in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting a food or diary entry in the app deletes it from your devices and iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Data StrongFive Food saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Analytics & Tracking

StrongFive Food contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, no advertising frameworks and no tracking. The app's only network connections are to Apple's iCloud and to Open Food Facts for barcode lookups.

Children

StrongFive Food is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
