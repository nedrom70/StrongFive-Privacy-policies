StrongFive Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

StrongFive is developed by Ice Digital. Your privacy is important to us.

Data Collection

StrongFive does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All workout data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal iCloud account using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage.

HealthKit

StrongFive requests access to Apple HealthKit to read your workout history, calories and heart rate data, and to write your completed workouts to the Health app. This data is used solely to display workout history and health metrics in the app and to log workouts to Health. HealthKit data is never sent to any server or shared with any third party.

Apple Watch

When paired with an Apple Watch, StrongFive streams heart rate and calorie data from the Watch to your iPhone during workouts. This data stays on your devices and is not transmitted externally.

iCloud Sync

Your program and workout history are synced via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage to your personal iCloud account. That storage has a size limit, so the iCloud copy holds your most recent workouts; each device keeps its full history locally. This data is governed by Apple's iCloud privacy policy.

Exporting Your Data

Settings → Export Data creates a file of your workout history on your device. The file leaves your device only when you choose to share it, for example by saving it or sending it with the share sheet.

Analytics & Tracking

StrongFive contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no advertising frameworks. There are zero network calls to any server other than Apple's iCloud.
