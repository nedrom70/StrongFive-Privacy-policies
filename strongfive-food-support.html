StrongFive Food Support

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Questions, a bug, or a food with the wrong numbers? Email support@icedigital.co. In the app, Settings → Contact Support opens a message with your app version filled in. On your Home Screen the app is called 5 Food.

Getting started

  • Set your daily goal: the first-run screens suggest calories, protein, carbs and fat from your goal (lose, maintain or gain), age, height, weight and activity. Tap Use Apple Health to fill these in. Change it any time in Settings → Daily Goal → Recalculate, or choose Enter My Own.
  • Log food: tap + on a meal. Search your foods and the built-in common foods, tap Search online for packaged foods, or scan a barcode. ⊕ logs a food at the portion you used last time.
  • A barcode isn't found: create the food yourself; you can photograph the Nutrition Facts table to fill it in.

Features

  • Saved meals: on a meal in your diary, tap ··· → Save as Meal. It then appears at the top of Add Food; one tap logs every item on its own line, and Undo removes them all.
  • Recipes: ··· → Save as Recipe logs a dish as one food, split into portions.
  • Flex day: for a birthday or a holiday dinner, touch and hold the summary at the top of the diary → Flex Day. It adds extra calories to that day only; your goal doesn't change. Remove it from the same menu.
  • Exercise calories: Settings → Add Exercise Calories adds the active energy Apple Health records to that day's calorie goal. It's off by default and syncs between your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Export: Settings → Export Data makes spreadsheet (CSV) files of your diary, weights, foods, goals and flex days.

Apple Watch

  • Install: open the Watch app on your iPhone → My Watch → scroll to Available Apps → Install next to 5 Food. Settings → Apple Watch in the app shows whether it's installed.
  • Watch face: touch and hold the watch face → Edit → swipe to Complications → pick a slot → 5 Food → Calories Left.
  • The watch face is behind: it updates within seconds when you log on your iPhone, or within about 30 minutes otherwise. Opening the 5 Food app on the Watch updates it at once.

Widgets

  • Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot → Edit → Add Widget → search for 5 Food → Calories Left (small or medium).
  • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the widget area → 5 Food.
  • A widget that says "Open 5 Food" fills in after you open the app once.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can log a food, log a saved meal, quick add calories or ask how many calories you have left. Open the Shortcuts app and search for 5 Food to see each action and the phrases Siri understands.

Apple Health

  • Turn on Settings → Save to Apple Health to save what you log and the weights you add.
  • To change what the app may read or write: Health app → your picture → Apps (Sharing) → 5 Food.
  • Numbers didn't fill in from Health? Check that height, date of birth and sex are in your Health details and that 5 Food may read them.

iCloud sync

  • Your diary syncs through your own iCloud account to your other devices and your Apple Watch. Settings → iCloud in the app shows whether sync is on.
  • Sync is off? Sign in to iCloud on each device (Settings → your name) and make sure iCloud is allowed for 5 Food (Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud → See All).
  • Changes usually arrive within a minute, sooner while the app is open. Use the same Apple Account on every device.

Your data and privacy

There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your devices, in your own iCloud and, if you allow it, in Apple Health. Read the StrongFive Food Privacy Policy for the details, including how to delete your data.
